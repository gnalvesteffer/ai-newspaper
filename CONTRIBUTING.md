# Contributing

The Daily Signal is proprietary and all rights are reserved under [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Gavin Alvesteffer controls commercial licensing. Public source availability does
not grant permission to use, redistribute, or commercialize the project.

Before accepting an outside contribution:

1. Identify the exact pull request, commits, and original assets covered.
2. Obtain a completed, signed [contribution rights assignment](CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md)
   from the rights owner (including an employer where applicable).
3. Review disclosed third-party material and confirm permission to include it.
4. Gavin Alvesteffer verifies the assignment and records approval on the PR.
   Do not merge an outside contribution until that approval is recorded.
5. Keep signed agreements privately; never commit personal signature documents.

Opening a PR or ticking a checkbox does not itself establish a rights transfer.
Existing contributions require an explicit assignment if the rights are not
already held by Gavin Alvesteffer. This process does not override third-party
licenses or previously granted permissions.

Agents and reviewers must follow the same acceptance requirement and the
repository's independent review instructions in `.agents/skills/`.
